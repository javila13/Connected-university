Whether you're starting your degree, advancing your career or adding a credential, Connected University has a path for you. Choose from {{facts.programs.undergraduate}} undergraduate programs, {{facts.programs.graduate}} graduate programs, {{facts.programs.onlineCoursesLabel}} online courses and {{facts.programs.certificates}} professional certificates through Continuing Education.

Our programs are organized into schools of Science & Sustainability, Business, Engineering & Computing, Nursing & Health Sciences, and Education & Human Development. Wherever you study, you'll learn in small classes from faculty who also advise students one-on-one and bring their research into the classroom.

## How we teach

- **Advising from day one.** Every student is matched with an advisor in their first semester, and faculty check in early when a course gets off to a rough start.
- **Learning by doing.** Labs, field work, clinical rotations, client projects and internships are built into every major.
- **Flexible formats.** Many programs are offered on campus, online or in a hybrid format, with the same faculty and the same degree.
