Your undergraduate years are where you find what you're passionate about and build the skills to do something with it. At Connected University you'll get the academic depth of a research university with the personal attention of a small college.

## What to expect

**First year.** You'll take foundation courses in writing, math and science alongside an introduction to your major, and meet your advisor in your first semester. Undecided? Exploratory Studies lets you try several fields before you choose.

**Second year.** You'll go deeper into your major, declare it if you haven't already, and start looking for research, internship and leadership opportunities.

**Third and fourth years.** Upper-level courses, research with faculty, internships and a capstone project or clinical placement bring everything together and prepare you for your career or graduate school.

## Support every step of the way

Advising, tutoring and career services are part of every undergraduate's experience, not something you have to go looking for. If a course gets hard, your faculty and advisor will reach out early so you can get back on track.
