Online learning at Connected University isn't a separate school or a watered-down version of the real thing. It's the same education, designed for people who need to learn on their own schedule.

## How online learning works

Courses are organized into weekly modules with readings, recorded lectures, discussions and assignments. You'll work at your own pace within each week, with clear deadlines and regular feedback from your instructor. Many courses also offer optional live sessions where you can ask questions and meet classmates.

## Support from anywhere

Online students get the same support as students on campus: a dedicated advisor, online tutoring, library services, career coaching and technical help. Everything is a click away in the Student Portal.

## Who studies online

Our online students include working adults finishing a degree, registered nurses earning a B.S.N., professionals pursuing a master's, and on-campus students who take an online course to fit a busy semester.
