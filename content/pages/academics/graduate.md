A graduate degree from Connected University is built around your life, not the other way around. Our master's programs combine advanced coursework with projects you can apply directly at work, taught by faculty who are active in their fields.

## Built for working professionals

Most graduate courses run online or in the evening, and many programs offer part-time and hybrid options. You'll learn alongside experienced classmates from across industries, and your projects will often solve real problems at your own organization.

## What sets our programs apart

- **Applied from day one.** Case studies, client projects and capstones tied to your career goals.
- **Faculty who practice.** Instructors bring current industry, clinical and classroom experience.
- **Support that fits your schedule.** Advising, library services and tutoring are available online and in the evening.
