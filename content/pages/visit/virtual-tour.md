Can't make it to campus yet? Take a walk through Connected University from wherever you are. When you're ready to see it in person, [book a campus tour](/visit/campus-tour/).

## Stop 1: The main quad

The heart of campus, shaded by old oaks and ringed by classroom buildings. On sunny days, and there are a lot of them, students study and eat lunch on the lawn.

## Stop 2: Science & Sustainability Hall

Home of Environmental Science, Sustainability Studies and Chemistry. Look for the teaching labs on the second floor, where Organic Chemistry students run their experiments, and Dr. Olga Martinez's research lab.

## Stop 3: The main library

One of Connected University's {{facts.research.libraries}} libraries, and home to the Tutoring & Learning Center on the second floor.

## Stop 4: Sustainability House

A living-learning community for students who care about the environment, with a rooftop garden that grows food for the campus food pantry.

## Stop 5: Student Center and dining hall

Where students eat, meet and plan. Student Government, the involvement fair and many club meetings happen here.

## Stop 6: Condor Arena and Telegraph Field

Home of the Condors. Basketball and volleyball play in Condor Arena, and soccer plays under the lights at Telegraph Field.
