## By car

Our campus is in the Los Angeles area, close to the I-5 and I-605 freeways. Use the address above in your map app, and follow signs for the Welcome Center.

## Parking

Visitor parking is free. Park in the Welcome Center lot, then check in at the Welcome Center for your tour or event. On event days, follow the signs and parking volunteers.

## By air

Los Angeles International Airport (LAX), Long Beach Airport (LGB) and John Wayne Airport in Orange County (SNA) all serve the area. Long Beach is usually the closest.

## By public transit

Regional bus lines stop near campus. Use your transit app to plan a trip to the address above.
