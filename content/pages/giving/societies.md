The **Connected Circle** brings together alumni, parents and friends who give ${{facts.giving.connectedCircle.member}} or more each year. Their gifts create a dependable base of support that students count on, year after year: scholarships that close the gap, tutoring that turns a hard semester around, and programs that connect students to their futures.

Already giving? Many Connected Circle members started with a first gift of a few hundred dollars. Doubling a first gift is one of the most meaningful steps a donor can take.

| Benefactor | ${{facts.giving.connectedCircle.benefactor}} or more |
| President's Council | ${{facts.giving.presidentsCouncil}} or more |

## Member benefits

- **Recognition** in the annual Honor Roll of Donors, unless you'd rather stay anonymous
- **An invitation to the Connected Circle Reception** with the President each spring
- **Updates from the students you support**, including notes from scholarship recipients
- **Behind-the-scenes events**, from lab tours to preview nights for campus performances

Leaders and Benefactors are also invited to an annual dinner with a dean, and President's Council members join the President for an evening on campus and can create a named scholarship.
