Every gift to Connected University connects a student to opportunity. {{facts.research.scholarshipRecipientsPercent}}% of our students receive scholarships, many of them funded by alumni and friends, and gifts also support tutoring, research, campus life and athletics.

## Where your gift goes

- **The Connected Fund** goes where the need is greatest each year, from emergency grants to new lab equipment.
- **Scholarships** help students afford a Connected University education. See [Scholarship Funds](/giving/scholarships/).
- **Student success** programs, like the Tutoring & Learning Center's study sessions for the toughest courses, help students stay on track to graduate.
- **Schools and programs**, from the School of Science & Sustainability to Nursing.

## Every gift counts

Gifts of every size add up. Last year, most gifts to the Connected Fund came from alumni giving what they could, and many were first gifts. When you're ready to do more, the [Connected Circle](/giving/societies/) brings together donors who give ${{facts.giving.connectedCircle.member}} or more each year.
