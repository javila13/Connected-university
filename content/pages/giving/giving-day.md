Once a year, alumni, students, parents, faculty, staff and friends come together for a single day of giving. Every gift, of any size, counts, and every gift helps unlock matching challenges that multiply the impact.

## How it works

- **One day.** Giving Day runs for one full day each March, from midnight to midnight.
- **Challenges and matches.** Donors pledge to match gifts when goals are reached: the most gifts from a graduating class, a team or a school.
- **A live leaderboard** shows which classes, teams and programs are leading.
- **Choose your cause.** Support the Connected Fund, scholarships, a program or a team you love.

## Become an ambassador

Ambassadors share Giving Day with their networks, classmates, teammates and colleagues, and help their class climb the leaderboard. It takes a few minutes and makes a big difference.

[Update your info](/alumni/update-info/) to get Giving Day reminders, or [give now](/giving/give/) to get a head start.
