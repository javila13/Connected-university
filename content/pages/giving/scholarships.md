{{facts.research.scholarshipRecipientsPercent}}% of Connected University students receive scholarships, and for many of them a scholarship is what makes enrollment possible. Every gift to a scholarship fund goes directly toward helping students pay for college.

## Featured funds

- **Sustainability Leaders Scholarship Fund** supports the Sustainability Leaders Scholarship, worth ${{facts.scholarships.sustainabilityLeaders}} a year, for Environmental Science and Sustainability Studies students who lead environmental projects. [Give to this fund](/giving/give/?designation=sustainability-leaders).
- **First-Generation Scholars Fund** supports students who are the first in their families to go to college. [Give to this fund](/giving/give/?designation=first-generation).
- **Nursing Scholarship Fund** helps future nurses focus on clinical training instead of extra work hours. [Give to this fund](/giving/give/?designation=nursing).
- **Scholarships** (general) go to the students with the greatest need each year. [Give to this fund](/giving/give/?designation=scholarships).

## Create a named scholarship

An endowed scholarship of ${{facts.giving.endowedScholarshipMinimum}} or more supports students in your name, or the name of someone you'd like to honor, forever. You can choose the program or the kind of student it supports, and you'll hear from the students who receive it.

## For students

Looking for scholarships to apply for? See [Scholarships](/admissions/affordability/scholarships/) in Admissions.
