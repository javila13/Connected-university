There's more than one way to support Connected University students. Choose the one that fits your plans, and contact the Office of Advancement if you'd like help.

## Give online

The fastest way to give. [Give now](/giving/give/) with a one-time or monthly gift, and choose where it goes.

## Give monthly

Monthly gifts spread your support across the year and give students a steady base of support. ${{facts.giving.connectedCircle.monthly}} a month makes you a member of the [Connected Circle](/giving/societies/).

## Matching gifts

Many employers match their employees' gifts, often dollar for dollar. Request a match through your employer, and your matched gift counts toward your giving society membership.

## Stock and securities

Giving appreciated stock or mutual funds can be a tax-smart way to give. Contact the Office of Advancement before you make the transfer.

## IRA and donor-advised funds

If you're eligible, a qualified charitable distribution from your IRA can count toward your required minimum distribution. You can also recommend a grant to Connected University from your donor-advised fund.

## Planned gifts

Include Connected University in your will or name it as a beneficiary of a retirement account or life insurance policy. See [Planned Giving](/giving/planned-giving/).

## By mail or phone

Mail a check payable to Connected University to the Office of Advancement, {{facts.campus.street}}, {{facts.campus.city}}, {{facts.campus.region}} {{facts.campus.postalCode}}, or call {{facts.campus.phone}}.

These options aren't tax advice. Talk with your financial or tax advisor about what's right for you.
