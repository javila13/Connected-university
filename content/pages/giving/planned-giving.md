A planned gift lets you support Connected University students for generations, often without changing your finances today. Many planned gifts take just a few minutes to arrange.

## Ways to make a planned gift

- **A gift in your will or trust.** Leave a specific amount, a percentage of your estate or what remains after other gifts.
- **A beneficiary designation.** Name Connected University as a beneficiary of a retirement account, life insurance policy or bank account.
- **A charitable gift annuity.** Make a gift and receive fixed payments for life.
- **A charitable remainder trust.** Receive income from your gift for life or a set term, with the remainder supporting students.

## The Legacy Society

Everyone who includes Connected University in their estate plans is welcome in the Legacy Society. Members are invited to an annual luncheon on campus and receive updates on the students and programs their future gift will support.
