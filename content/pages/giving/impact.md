Gifts to Connected University show up in real students' lives: a scholarship that makes enrollment possible, a study session that turns a hard semester around, a project that changes a neighborhood. Here are a few of them.

## A second chance in chemistry

Organic Chemistry is the course where many science majors decide whether they belong. Donor gifts to the **Tutoring & Learning Center** fund weekly study sessions for it, led by students who recently aced the course.

Jordan Reyes, an Environmental Science major, withdrew from Organic Chemistry after a rough start and nearly changed majors. Jordan's advisor connected Jordan to the study sessions, and the next term Jordan retook the course and passed. "Those sessions are why I'm still a science major," Jordan says.

## First in the family

The **First-Generation Scholars Fund** supports students who are the first in their families to go to college. Amara Osei, a Nursing student, says the scholarship meant working fewer hours and spending more time in the simulation lab. Amara plans to work in community health after graduation.

## A garden on the roof

Alumni from the Sustainability Alumni Network funded the rooftop garden at **Sustainability House**, where students grow vegetables for the campus food pantry and run workshops for local schools.

## When the unexpected happens

Gifts to the **Connected Fund** support emergency grants for students facing a sudden crisis, like a family job loss or an unexpected bill. A small grant at the right moment can be the difference between staying enrolled and stopping out.
