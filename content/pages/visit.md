The best way to know if Connected University is right for you is to see it. Tour our campus with a personalized visit tailored to your academic interests, learn about the extra amenities Connected U students receive on campus, and see what a day in the life of a student looks like in person.

## Plan your visit

- **[Campus Tour](/visit/campus-tour/):** a student-led walking tour, weekdays and Saturdays. Add an information session, a personal visit with a counselor or a class visit.
- **[Virtual Tour](/visit/virtual-tour/):** can't make it in person? Start here.
- **[Directions & Parking](/visit/directions/):** how to get here and where to park.

Coming for a big day? See [Events](/events/) for Open House and Admitted Students Day.
