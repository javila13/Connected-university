Connected University is a community of educators, researchers and professionals who share one goal: helping students become the leaders of tomorrow. If that sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.

## Who we hire

- **Faculty** who love teaching, advise students one-on-one and bring their research and practice into the classroom
- **Staff** in advising, admissions, financial aid, student life, technology, facilities, athletics and advancement
- **Student employees** in labs, libraries, offices and the Tutoring & Learning Center

## Why work here

- **Tuition remission** for eligible employees and their families
- **Health, retirement and wellness benefits**, including access to the recreation center
- **Professional development**, including discounted professional certificates through Continuing Education
- **A mission you can see**, every day, in the students you help

## Our commitment

Connected University is an equal opportunity employer. We welcome applicants of every background and are committed to a campus where everyone belongs.
