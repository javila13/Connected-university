Connected University is led by a president and a senior leadership team, guided by an independent Board of Trustees.

## President

**Dr. Catherine Alvarado-Shaw** became Connected University's president after a career as a professor of public health and a dean of student success. Dr. Alvarado-Shaw's priorities are making sure every student who enrolls graduates, expanding pathways for working adults and alumni to keep learning, and deepening the university's partnerships across Southern California.

## Senior leadership

- **Dr. Neil Barrington**, Provost and Vice President for Academic Affairs, leads the schools, the faculty and the academic programs.
- **Dr. Monique Fairbanks**, Vice President for Student Success, oversees advising, tutoring, health and wellness, career services and campus life, including the early alert program.
- **Marisa Delgado**, Vice President for Enrollment Management, leads admissions and financial aid.
- **Thomas Reilly-Chen**, Vice President for Advancement, leads alumni relations, fundraising and Giving Day.
- **Dr. Andrea Lindqvist**, Dean of Continuing Education, leads professional certificates, corporate training and Return to CU.

## Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees sets the university's direction and oversees its finances. Trustees include alumni, business and community leaders, and faculty and student representatives.
