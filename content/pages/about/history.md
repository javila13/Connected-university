Connected University was founded in {{facts.founded}} as a small teachers college serving the growing towns south and east of Los Angeles. Its first students were mostly the first in their families to go to college, and they came to become teachers in their own communities.

## Growing with Southern California

As the region grew, so did the college. Programs in nursing, business and engineering followed in the decades after World War II, as local hospitals, companies and cities looked for graduates who understood the region. The college became a university, and a research university not long after, with libraries and labs to match.

## Connected by design

The university took the name Connected University to reflect what had always made it different: the connections between students and faculty, between campus and community, and between graduates and the students who came after them. Advising from the first semester, early alerts when a course gets hard, and an alumni network that comes back to mentor students all grew from that idea.

## Looking ahead

A century after its founding, Connected University offers {{facts.programs.undergraduate}} undergraduate and {{facts.programs.graduate}} graduate programs, online learning and professional certificates for working adults, and a growing commitment to sustainability across every school. Its mission hasn't changed: to provide students with the best connections, education and experience to be the leaders of tomorrow.
