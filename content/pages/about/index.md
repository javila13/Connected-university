Connected University exists to connect students to their future. Our mission is to provide our students with the best connections, education, and experience to be the leaders of tomorrow.

## Connect to Opportunity, Passion and Community

Every program, service and tradition here is designed around three connections: to **opportunity** through alumni and professionals in your field, to **passion** through the interests closest to your heart, and to **community** through friendships and colleagues that last a lifetime.
