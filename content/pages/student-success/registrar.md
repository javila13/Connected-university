## Registering for classes

Registration opens in order of class standing, so seniors register first. Meet with your advisor before your registration date, and check the Student Portal for any holds that could stop you from registering.

## Dropping, withdrawing and retaking

Life happens. If a course isn't working, you have options:

- **Drop** it by {{facts.registrar.addDrop}} and it won't appear on your record.
- **Withdraw** by {{facts.registrar.withdraw}} and you'll receive a W, which doesn't affect your GPA.
- **Retake** it in a later term. The new grade replaces the earlier one in your GPA.

Talk with your advisor first. Together you can decide whether to stay in the course with extra support or take a fresh start.

## Transcripts and verification

Order official transcripts and enrollment verification letters in the Student Portal. Graduating students apply to graduate in the portal the term before their final term.
