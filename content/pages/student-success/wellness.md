You learn best when you're well. Health and wellness services at Connected University are here for your body, your mind and the everyday basics.

**If you're in crisis, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline at any time, or call 911 in an emergency.**

## Student Health Center

The Student Health Center offers primary care, sick visits, immunizations and health education. Book appointments in the [Student Portal](/portal/), or walk in for urgent concerns.

## Counseling

Free, confidential counseling helps with stress, anxiety, relationships, grief, identity and anything else on your mind. Choose individual sessions, groups or workshops, in person or by video.

## Recreation and fitness

The recreation center has a fitness floor, a pool, courts and group classes, all included in your student fees. Club and intramural sports are open to every student, whatever your skill level.

## Basic needs

No one should have to choose between food and studying. The campus food pantry is open to every student, and the Dean of Students office can help with emergency grants, housing insecurity and other urgent needs.

## Accessibility Services

Students with disabilities, including learning differences, chronic illness and mental health conditions, can request accommodations through Accessibility Services.
