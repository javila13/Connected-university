College is more than classes. Campus life at Connected University is where you'll meet friends, find what you care about and build connections for a lifetime.

## Housing

First-year students live in residence halls designed for community, with study lounges on every floor and resident advisors who help you settle in. Upper-level students can choose apartment-style housing.

Want to live with people who share your interests? Living-learning communities bring together students around a theme. **Sustainability House** is home to students who care about the environment, with shared projects, a rooftop garden and events with Science & Sustainability faculty.

Admitted students apply for housing in the [Student Portal](/portal/).

## Dining

The main dining hall serves fresh, made-to-order meals all day, and cafés across campus keep you going between classes. Much of our produce comes from regional farms, and every location labels vegetarian, vegan and allergen-free options.

## Clubs and organizations

Whatever you're into, there's a group for it, and if there isn't, you can start one. Popular choices include:

- **Environmental Action Coalition**, which runs campus clean-ups and the annual sustainability fair
- **Pre-Health Society**, for students headed to medical, nursing and health careers
- **Business & Entrepreneurship Club**, with a pitch competition every spring
- **Student Government**, which represents students and funds new clubs
- **Club and intramural sports**, for every skill level

## Southern California at your doorstep

Our Santa Fe Springs campus puts beaches, mountains and the arts, food and culture of Los Angeles within easy reach.
