Connect to Opportunity starts on day one. Career Services helps you explore what you want to do, build the experience to get there and connect with the people who can help.

## What we offer

- **Career coaching** to explore majors and careers, and to plan your next step
- **Internships and research** with regional employers, nonprofits, public agencies and faculty labs
- **Career fairs** every fall and spring, plus industry nights for specific fields
- **Résumé, cover letter and interview prep**, including practice interviews
- **A job board** in the Student Portal for internships, on-campus jobs and full-time roles

## Learn from alumni

Connected University alumni work across industries and come back to help. Join mentoring programs, informational interviews and alumni panels to learn how they got where they are.

## For every major

Career coaches specialize by field, so an Environmental Science student and a Business student each get advice that fits their path. Many programs also build internships and capstone projects into the degree. See your [program page](/academics/programs/) for details.
