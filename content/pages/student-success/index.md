Success at Connected University is a team effort. Your academic advisor, the Tutoring & Learning Center, health and wellness services and career services work together, so you always know where to turn and someone always notices if you need help.

## We reach out first

You don't have to be in crisis to get support, and you don't have to ask first. When an instructor sees signs that a course is getting hard, such as missed labs, a low exam score or a dropped class, they raise an **early alert**. Your advisor follows up within days by email, and by text if you've opted in, to make a plan with you: tutoring, a schedule change or a fresh start in a later term.

Many of our most successful graduates had a semester that didn't go to plan. What made the difference was getting help early.

## Everything in one place

Sign in to the [Student Portal](/portal/) to see your courses, your advisor, any alerts and the deadlines coming up this term.
