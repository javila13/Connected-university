Everyone needs help sometimes, and the strongest students are often the ones who ask for it early. The Tutoring & Learning Center, on the second floor of the main library, offers free support in almost every subject.

## How we help

- **Drop-in tutoring** in math, chemistry, biology, physics, statistics and programming, no appointment needed
- **One-on-one appointments** with a trained peer tutor, booked through the Student Portal
- **Weekly study sessions** for challenging courses, led by students who recently aced them
- **The Writing Center**, for help at any stage of a paper, from brainstorming to final edits
- **Study skills coaching** on time management, note-taking and test preparation

## Study sessions for gateway courses

Some courses, like Organic Chemistry and Calculus, are where many students decide whether a major is right for them. Our weekly study sessions for these courses are led by students who took them recently and know where people get stuck. Students who attend regularly tend to earn higher grades, and many who retake a course make the sessions part of their plan.

## Working with your advisor

If your advisor or instructor raises an early alert, tutoring is often part of the plan. You can book sessions yourself in the [Student Portal](/portal/), and with your permission your tutor can share your progress with your advisor.
