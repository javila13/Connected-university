Every Connected University student is matched with a faculty advisor in their first semester. Most students keep the same advisor through graduation, so your advisor gets to know your goals, your strengths and the semesters that were harder than others.

## What your advisor helps with

- Planning your courses each term and mapping out your path to graduation
- Choosing or changing a major, adding a minor or studying abroad
- Finding research projects, internships and leadership roles
- Preparing for graduate school or your first job
- Making a plan when a course or a semester gets hard

## Early alerts

Instructors raise an early alert when they see signs a student may need support, such as missed labs, a low exam score or a dropped course. Here's what happens next:

1. **Your advisor reaches out** within days, by email and, if you've opted in, by text.
2. **You make a plan together.** That might mean tutoring, a lighter course load, or withdrawing from a course and retaking it when you're ready.
3. **Your support team follows up** through the rest of the term to make sure the plan is working.

An early alert isn't a penalty. It's how we make sure no one falls through the cracks.

## Before your appointment

Review your degree progress in the [Student Portal](/portal/), list the courses you're considering for next term, and bring your questions. Not sure what to ask? Start with "Am I on track to graduate when I want to?"
