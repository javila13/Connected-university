Continuing Education at Connected University helps working professionals build new skills on their schedule. Earn one of {{facts.programs.certificates}} professional certificates, bring training to your team, or come back to finish what you started.

## Three ways to keep learning

- **Professional certificates** in sustainability, project management, data analytics, leadership, cybersecurity, nonprofit management and more. See the [certificate catalog](/continuing-ed/certificates/).
- **Corporate training** for teams, from custom workshops to private certificate cohorts. See [Corporate Training](/continuing-ed/corporate-training/).
- **A way back** for alumni and former students: {{facts.alumni.ceDiscountPercent}}% off certificates for graduates, certificate credits that count toward a master's degree, and help finishing a bachelor's degree. See [Return to CU](/continuing-ed/return/).

## Built for working adults

Courses run one at a time, mostly online, with weekly deadlines rather than set class times. You'll learn from Connected University faculty and practitioners, and finish every course with a project you can use at work.
