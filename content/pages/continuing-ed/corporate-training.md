Your people are your advantage. Connected University Continuing Education works with employers of every size to build skills where they're needed most: leadership, project management, data, sustainability, cybersecurity and more.

## How we work with employers

- **Custom programs.** Workshops and short courses designed around your goals, your industry and your schedule.
- **Private certificate cohorts.** Your team earns a professional certificate together, with projects built around your organization.
- **Tuition partnerships.** Direct billing and group pricing for employees who enroll in our open certificates or degree programs.
- **Flexible delivery.** Online, on our Santa Fe Springs campus or at your workplace.

## Popular topics

Leadership and coaching, project management, data analytics, sustainability strategy and reporting, cybersecurity awareness, and nonprofit board development. See the full [certificate catalog](/continuing-ed/certificates/).
