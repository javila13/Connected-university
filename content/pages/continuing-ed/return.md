Once a Connected U student, always a Connected U student. Whether you graduated last year or left before finishing, there's a way back that fits your life now.

## Add a credential

Connected University graduates save **{{facts.alumni.ceDiscountPercent}}%** on every professional certificate. It's the fastest way to build new skills for your next role, and many alumni use a certificate to test the waters before committing to a degree.

## Turn your certificate into a master's degree

Many certificates earn graduate credits that count toward a Connected University master's degree. Already earned the Professional Certificate in Sustainability? You're part of the way to the online MBA with a Sustainable Business focus.

<!-- certificate-pathways -->

Ready to talk it through? [Request information](/continuing-ed/inquiry/?interest=masters) and an advisor will show you exactly which credits count.

## Finish your bachelor's degree

Life gets in the way sometimes. If you left Connected University before finishing your degree, the credits you earned still count. A returning-student advisor will review your record, show you what's left and help you plan a schedule, with online options for many programs. [Let us know you're interested](/continuing-ed/inquiry/?interest=finish-degree).
