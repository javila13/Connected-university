Connected University offers {{facts.programs.certificates}} professional certificates for people who want new skills without putting their careers on hold. Courses run one at a time, and you can study online or, for some certificates, in a hybrid format. Each certificate page lists its length, start dates and tuition.

Every certificate is taught by Connected University faculty and practitioners, and many earn graduate credits that count toward a master's degree.
