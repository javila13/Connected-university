About to finish community college, or ready to change schools? Transfer students are a big part of Connected University, and we make it simple to bring your credits with you.

## How your credits transfer

- **Up to {{facts.admissions.transfer.maxCredits}} credits.** Courses from accredited colleges with a grade of C or better are eligible.
- **General education.** If you've completed an associate degree for transfer, your general education requirements are typically complete.
- **Major courses.** We match your courses to ours, and faculty review anything that needs a closer look.
- **A clear plan.** Your credit evaluation comes with your admission decision and shows how many credits you'll need to graduate.

## Get a credit estimate

Not sure how your credits will count? [Request information](/admissions/request-information/?type=transfer-student) as a transfer student and your admissions counselor will give you an unofficial estimate before you apply.

## What you'll need

- Official transcripts from every college you've attended
- A personal statement about your goals
- Your high school transcript, if you've completed less than one year of college

## Scholarships for transfer students

The **Transfer Achievement Scholarship** awards up to ${{facts.scholarships.transferAchievement}} a year to transfer students with strong college grades. Every transfer applicant is considered automatically. See [Scholarships](/admissions/affordability/scholarships/) for more.
