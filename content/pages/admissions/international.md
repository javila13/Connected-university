Students from around the world come to Connected University for our programs, our faculty and our Southern California campus. We'll help you every step of the way, from your application and student visa to your first week on campus.

## How to apply

International students apply as [first-year](/admissions/undergraduate/), [transfer](/admissions/transfer/) or [graduate](/admissions/graduate/) students, with a few extra steps:

- **Academic records** from secondary school or college, with certified English translations if needed
- **Proof of English proficiency**, if English isn't your first language
- **Financial documents** showing you can cover your first year of study, so we can issue the I-20 you need for your student visa

## Support when you arrive

International Student Services helps with visas, travel, housing, banking and settling in. You'll start with an international student orientation before classes begin, and you'll have an advisor who checks in throughout your first year.
