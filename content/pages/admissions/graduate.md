Our master's programs are built for working professionals, and our admission process is too. Graduate programs use **rolling admission**: we review your application as soon as it's complete, so you can apply when you're ready.

Applications that arrive after the priority date are reviewed if space remains.

## What you'll need

- A bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university
- Official transcripts from every college you've attended
- A résumé
- A statement of purpose about your goals and why the program fits them
- Letters of recommendation from people who know your work
- Program-specific items, such as an active RN license for the M.S.N.

## Paying for your degree

Graduate tuition is ${{facts.costs.graduatePerCredit}} per credit. Graduate students can apply for federal financial aid, and many programs offer scholarships and graduate assistantships. Ask your employer about tuition assistance, too; many of our students use it.
