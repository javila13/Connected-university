We read every application as a whole. Grades matter, but so do the classes you chose, how you've grown, what you care about and what you'd bring to our community. There's no formula, and no single number decides.

## What we look for

- **Your academic record.** The courses you've taken, your grades and how they've changed over time.
- **Your personal statement.** What you care about and how you think, in your own voice.
- **Your activities.** Jobs, family responsibilities, clubs, sports, service and creative work all count.
- **A recommendation** from a teacher or counselor who knows you well.

Connected University is **test-optional**. You may send SAT or ACT scores if you think they add to your application, and you won't be at a disadvantage if you don't.

## How to apply

1. [Start your application](/admissions/apply/). It's free.
2. Ask your school to send your high school transcript.
3. Write your personal statement and list your activities.
4. Ask a teacher or counselor for a recommendation.
5. Submit by your deadline, then track your status in the Student Portal.
