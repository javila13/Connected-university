## Your financial aid offer

Your aid offer lists your scholarships, grants and estimated net cost. If you have questions, or your family's situation has changed, a financial aid counselor will walk through it with you. See [Affordability](/admissions/affordability/) for how aid works.

## Admitted Students Day

Every April we invite admitted students and their families to campus for a day of classes, campus tours and conversations with faculty and current students. It's the best way to picture yourself here.
