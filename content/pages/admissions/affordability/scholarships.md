## Field-of-study scholarships

The **Sustainability Leaders Scholarship** awards ${{facts.scholarships.sustainabilityLeaders}} a year to students in [Environmental Science](/academics/programs/environmental-science/) or [Sustainability Studies](/academics/programs/sustainability-studies/) who have led environmental projects in their school or community. Add a short essay to your application to be considered.

## Donor-funded scholarships

Alumni and friends of Connected University fund hundreds of named scholarships each year. They're matched to students by major, background and interests, and most require no separate application.
