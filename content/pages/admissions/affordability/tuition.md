## Applying for aid

Submit the FAFSA by **{{facts.admissions.deadlines.aidPriority}}** for priority consideration for need-based grants, work-study and federal loans. Your aid offer, with your estimated net cost, appears in the Student Portal.

## Paying your bill

Bills are posted to the Student Portal before each semester. You can pay in full or use our interest-free monthly payment plan, and you can give a parent or guardian access to view and pay your bill.
