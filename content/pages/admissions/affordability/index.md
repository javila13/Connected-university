The price of college and what you actually pay are rarely the same. **{{facts.research.scholarshipRecipientsPercent}}% of Connected University students receive scholarships**, and most also receive grants and other aid that never has to be repaid.

## How financial aid works

1. **Apply for admission.** You're considered for merit scholarships automatically.
2. **Submit the FAFSA** by **{{facts.admissions.deadlines.aidPriority}}** to be considered for need-based grants, work-study and loans.
3. **Review your aid offer** in the Student Portal. It shows your scholarships, grants and your estimated net cost.
4. **Ask questions.** Financial aid counselors meet with families in person, by phone or by video.

## Types of aid

- **Scholarships** for academic achievement, leadership and specific fields of study
- **Grants** from Connected University, your state and the federal government, based on financial need
- **Work-study** jobs on campus, often in labs, libraries and offices related to your major
- **Loans** with clear terms, which we'll help you keep to a minimum
