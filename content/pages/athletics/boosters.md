Behind every Condors win is the **Condor Club**, the booster club for Connected University athletics. Gifts to the **Athletics Fund** support scholarships for student-athletes, team travel, equipment and the sports medicine staff who keep athletes healthy.

## Condor Club levels

| Level | Annual gift | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fan | ${{facts.athletics.boosterLevels.fan}} | Condor Club decal and the booster newsletter |
| Flight Crew | ${{facts.athletics.boosterLevels.flightCrew}} | Plus a Condor Club T-shirt and preseason open-practice invitations |
| Captain | ${{facts.athletics.boosterLevels.captain}} | Plus two all-sports passes |
| Champion | ${{facts.athletics.boosterLevels.champion}} | Plus reserved seating at basketball and the annual Champions Dinner with coaches |

## Where your gift goes

- **Scholarships** for student-athletes who balance competition and a full course load
- **Travel** so teams can compete across the region
- **Equipment and facilities**, like the new dugouts and bullpens at Founders Park
- **Sports medicine**, from athletic trainers to recovery equipment

## Join today

[Give to the Athletics Fund](/giving/give/?designation=athletics) and choose the level that's right for you. Every gift to the Athletics Fund also counts toward your [Connected Circle](/giving/societies/) membership.
