Connected University athletics brings students, alumni and the community together. The **{{facts.athletics.mascot}}** compete in the {{facts.athletics.conference}}, and our student-athletes are as committed in the classroom and the community as they are in competition.

## Catch a game

Home games at Condor Arena, Telegraph Field and Founders Park are some of the best nights on campus. Students get in free with their ID. See the [full schedule](/athletics/schedule/) and [get tickets](/athletics/tickets/).

## Join the Condor Club

The Condor Club is the booster club for Connected University athletics. Gifts to the Athletics Fund support scholarships, travel, equipment and sports medicine for every team. [Learn more](/athletics/boosters/).

## Why the condor?

The California condor is native to our part of the world and one of conservation's great comeback stories. Like the birds, our teams are built to go the distance.
