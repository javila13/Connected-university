Condors teams train and compete in facilities built for athletes and fans alike, all within a short walk of each other on our Santa Fe Springs campus.

## Condor Arena

Home of Men's and Women's Basketball and Women's Volleyball. The arena's student section, known as the Nest, wraps the baseline closest to the Condors bench, and a center-hung video board keeps every seat in the action.

## Telegraph Field

Home of Men's and Women's Soccer. The lit natural-grass field hosts night games all fall, with a grassy hillside for families and a pregame tailgate lawn.

## Founders Park

Home of Baseball. Founders Park's new dugouts and bullpens were funded by gifts to the Condor Club, and the shaded grandstand makes it one of the best places on campus to spend a spring afternoon.

## Sports Performance Center

Every Condors athlete trains here. The center includes a strength and conditioning floor, an athletic training and sports medicine clinic, recovery rooms and a study hall where student-athletes meet with tutors and academic advisors.

## For every student

The Recreation & Fitness Center, open to every Connected University student, has a fitness floor, a pool, courts and group classes, and it's home to club and intramural sports.
