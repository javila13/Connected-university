faqs:
  - q: Do students need a ticket?
    a: Connected University students get in free with their student ID. You can also reserve a free student ticket below to save a spot for big games.
  - q: Is there a season pass?
    a: Yes. The all-sports pass, ${{facts.athletics.allSportsPass}}, gets you into every regular-season home game for every team.
  - q: Where do I park?
    a: Parking is free for home games in the lots next to Condor Arena and Telegraph Field.
  - q: Can I get a refund?
    a: If a game is canceled, your tickets are refunded automatically. Otherwise, tickets can be exchanged for another home game of the same team.
