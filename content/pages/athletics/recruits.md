Want to compete as a Condor? Our coaches are always looking for student-athletes who are committed to their sport, their studies and their teammates.

## How recruiting works

1. **Fill out the recruiting questionnaire** below. It goes to the coaching staff for your sport.
2. **Send film and your schedule.** Coaches watch highlight videos and attend showcases and tournaments.
3. **Visit campus.** Tour our facilities, meet the team and sit in on a class.
4. **Apply for admission** through [Undergraduate Admission](/admissions/undergraduate/) and complete any eligibility requirements for your sport.

## Built for student-athletes

Condors student-athletes have their own academic advisors, tutoring and study hall in the Sports Performance Center, and sports medicine staff who keep them healthy. Our teams post some of the highest grade point averages on campus.
