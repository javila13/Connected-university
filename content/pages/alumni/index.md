Graduation isn't goodbye. You're one of {{facts.alumni.countLabel}} Connected University alumni, part of a lifelong community with reunions and events, a professional network that opens doors, benefits reserved for graduates, and stories of alumni making a difference.

## Stay connected

- **Events and reunions:** Homecoming & Reunion Weekend every October, plus regional gatherings and Young Alumni nights.
- **The alumni network:** chapters, industry networks, mentoring and Career Services for life.
- **Benefits:** {{facts.alumni.ceDiscountPercent}}% off Continuing Education certificates, library access, a digital alumni card and more.
- **Spotlights:** graduates making a difference, including award winners Wilma Grace and Courtney Tran-Kim.

## Keep us posted

New job, new city or new email? [Update your info](/alumni/update-info/) so we can keep you connected, and tell us what you'd like to hear about.
