Graduation isn't goodbye. All year, on campus and in cities across the country, Connected University alumni get together to reconnect, learn and give back.

## Homecoming & Reunion Weekend

Every October, alumni come home for a weekend of campus tours, faculty talks, the homecoming game, family activities and class reunion dinners. Milestone reunion classes, every {{facts.alumni.reunionEveryYears}} years after graduation, get their own celebrations, and every graduate is welcome.

## Regional gatherings

Alumni chapters host receptions, service days and casual meetups in Los Angeles, the San Francisco Bay Area, San Diego, Phoenix, Seattle, Denver, Chicago and New York, with new chapters forming as alumni volunteers step up.

## Young Alumni

Graduated in the last {{facts.alumni.youngAlumniYears}} years? Young Alumni events, from happy hours to career panels, are an easy way to stay close to classmates and meet alumni a few steps ahead of you.

## Learn and grow

Monthly career webinars cover topics from salary negotiation to changing fields, and faculty lectures bring the latest research to alumni online.
