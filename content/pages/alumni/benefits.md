Your connection to Connected University doesn't end at graduation. These benefits are free for every graduate, with no dues.

## Keep learning

- **{{facts.alumni.ceDiscountPercent}}% off professional certificates** through [Continuing Education](/continuing-ed/), from sustainability to project management and data analytics
- **Library access** to online journals and databases, plus borrowing privileges on campus
- **Faculty lectures and webinars** throughout the year

## Grow your career

- **Career Services for life**: coaching, résumé reviews and the alumni job board
- **The alumni network**: regional chapters, industry networks and mentoring. See [Alumni Network & Careers](/alumni/network/).

## Stay connected

- **Alumni events**, including Homecoming & Reunion Weekend. See [Events & Reunions](/alumni/events/).
- **A digital alumni card** for campus discounts at the bookstore and recreation center
- **The alumni newsletter** with news from campus and classmates

## Get your benefits

Most benefits start with up-to-date contact information. [Update your info](/alumni/update-info/) and we'll send your digital alumni card.
