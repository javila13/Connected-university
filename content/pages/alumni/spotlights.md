Connected University graduates are designing greener cities, caring for patients, building technology and teaching the next generation. Here are a few of their stories.

## Wilma Grace (YSS ’18) and Courtney Tran-Kim (YSS ’22)

**Gracelines International Sustainability Award winners**

Wilma Grace and Courtney Tran-Kim, both graduates of [Sustainability Studies](/academics/programs/sustainability-studies/), won the Gracelines International Sustainability Award for their work in sustainability. Wilma Grace leads community climate-resilience projects with city governments, and Courtney Tran-Kim helps companies measure and report their environmental impact. Both still come back to campus to talk with students in Foundations of Sustainability, the course where each of them started.

## Daniel Okafor ’09, Engineering

**Civil engineer, regional water district**

Daniel Okafor designs stormwater systems that capture rain instead of sending it out to sea. Daniel's senior design team built a rain garden on campus that still filters runoff today, and Daniel now mentors civil engineering students through the alumni network.

## Rosa Jimenez ’15, Nursing (B.S.N.) and ’21 (M.S.N.)

**Nurse educator, regional medical center**

Rosa Jimenez started as a bedside nurse in an emergency department and came back to Connected University for the online M.S.N. while working full time. Today Rosa Jimenez trains new graduate nurses and teaches clinical skills in the hospital's simulation lab.

## Kevin Lu ’19, Computer Science

**Machine learning engineer**

Kevin Lu builds machine learning systems that forecast energy demand for a renewable energy company, work that started in a faculty research group as an undergraduate. Kevin Lu speaks every year at the Computer Science career night.
