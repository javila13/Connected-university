{{facts.alumni.countLabel}} Connected University alumni live and work around the world. Wherever you are and whatever you do, there's someone in the network who's been where you're going.

## Find your people

- **Regional chapters** in cities across the country host events and service projects.
- **Industry networks** connect alumni in business, technology, health care, education, engineering and sustainability. The **Sustainability Alumni Network** is one of the most active, bringing together Environmental Science and Sustainability Studies graduates.
- **Affinity groups** bring together alumni with shared backgrounds, identities and interests.

## Career support for life

Connected University graduates can use [Career Services](/student-success/career-services/) for free, for life:

- Career coaching for job searches, promotions and career changes
- Résumé and LinkedIn profile reviews
- The job board, where employers post roles for Connected U alumni
- Career webinars on topics from negotiation to leadership

## Mentor a student

Some of the most valuable advice students get comes from alumni. Mentors meet with a student a few times a term, by video or in person, to talk about careers, internships and life after graduation. It's a small commitment that can change someone's path. [Tell us you're interested](/alumni/update-info/).
