Every organization, from a startup to a hospital to a city government, needs people who can plan, lead and make sound decisions with limited resources. Business Administration at Connected University gives you that foundation and lets you shape it around the career you want.

## Bachelor of Science

The B.S. starts with the core of business: accounting, marketing, finance, operations and management. By your third year you'll choose a concentration in Marketing, Finance, Management, Entrepreneurship or Sustainable Business, and take on a case competition, a consulting project or an internship with a regional employer.

## MBA

The MBA is built for working professionals. Courses run in eight-week terms, online or in a hybrid format, so you can keep working while you study. You'll finish with a strategy capstone for a real organization, often your own employer.

## What you'll learn

- How to read financial statements and build a budget
- How to research a market and position a product
- How to lead teams and manage change
- How to use data to make and defend business decisions
- How sustainable practices create long-term value
