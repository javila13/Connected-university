Sustainability is no longer a niche. Companies report on it, cities plan around it, and communities expect it. Sustainability Studies prepares you to lead that work: to understand the science, weigh the trade-offs and bring people together around solutions that last.

The program is interdisciplinary by design. You'll take courses in environmental science, economics, policy and communication, then put them to work on real projects with local businesses, agencies and nonprofits.

## What you'll learn

- The science behind climate, energy, water and materials
- How organizations measure and report their environmental and social impact
- How policy and planning decisions get made, and how to influence them
- How to lead community engagement that includes everyone affected
- Project management and persuasive communication

## Learning by doing

In the Community Partnership Practicum you'll join a team working on a live project for a local partner, such as an energy audit for a small business, a tree-canopy plan for a neighborhood or a waste-reduction program for a school district. Your capstone builds on that experience.

## Award-winning graduates

Wilma Grace (YSS ’18) and Courtney Tran-Kim (YSS ’22) won the Gracelines International Sustainability Award for their work in sustainability. Both started where you would: in Foundations of Sustainability.
