Nurses are the heart of health care, and the need for them keeps growing. The School of Nursing & Health Sciences prepares you to deliver safe, compassionate care and to lead change in hospitals, clinics, schools and communities.

## Three paths

- **B.S.N.** for students starting their nursing education. You'll build a foundation in the sciences, then learn in our simulation lab and in clinical rotations across the region.
- **RN to B.S.N.** for licensed registered nurses who want a bachelor's degree. It's fully online and designed around working schedules.
- **M.S.N.** for experienced nurses moving into education or leadership roles.

## Learn in the simulation lab

Before you care for real patients, you'll practice in a simulation lab with high-fidelity patient manikins that breathe, speak and respond to treatment. Faculty debrief every scenario with you so you learn from mistakes before they matter.

## What you'll learn

- Patient assessment and evidence-based clinical care
- Safe medication administration and patient safety
- Care across the lifespan, from newborns to older adults
- Community and public health
- Communication and teamwork with patients, families and care teams
