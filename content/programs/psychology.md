Psychology helps you understand people: why we make the choices we do, how we learn and grow, and what helps us when we struggle. It's one of the most flexible majors you can choose, useful in health care, business, education, technology and public service.

## What you'll learn

- The major areas of psychology, from brain and behavior to social and developmental psychology
- How to design a study, collect data and analyze it with statistics
- How mental health conditions are understood and treated
- How to write and present research clearly
- Ethical practice when working with people and their data

## Research and experience

Starting in your second year you can join a faculty research lab studying topics like memory, motivation and wellbeing. Many students also complete a supervised field placement with a community agency, school or health care provider before graduation.
