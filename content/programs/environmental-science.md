Clean water, healthy soil and breathable air don't take care of themselves. Environmental Science at Connected University teaches you how natural systems work, how people change them, and how to gather the evidence that leads to better decisions.

You'll spend as much time outdoors and in the lab as you do in the classroom. From your first semester you'll collect water and soil samples, learn the instruments professionals use, and turn raw data into findings a city council or a company can act on.

## What you'll learn

- How ecosystems, the water cycle and the atmosphere interact
- Field sampling, lab analysis and quality control
- Mapping and data analysis with GIS
- How environmental laws and permits work, and how science informs them
- How to communicate findings to the public, to industry and to policymakers

## Beyond the classroom

Every Environmental Science major completes a senior capstone: a real project for a community partner, a public agency or a faculty research lab. Along the way you can intern with environmental consulting firms, water districts and conservation nonprofits, or join faculty research on water quality and green chemistry.

## An advisor from day one

Every major is matched with a faculty advisor in their first semester and keeps that advisor through graduation. Advisors help you plan your courses, find research and internships, and get support quickly if a class gets hard. [Dr. Martinez](/academics/faculty/martinez/), who teaches Organic Chemistry, advises many of our Environmental Science majors.
