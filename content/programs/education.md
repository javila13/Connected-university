Great teachers change lives. The School of Education & Human Development prepares educators who know their subject, understand how people learn, and can make every student feel they belong.

## Bachelor of Arts in Education Studies

You'll study how children and adolescents learn, how to teach reading, math and science, and how to design classrooms that work for every learner. Choose the Teacher Preparation pathway if you plan to teach, or the Learning & Community pathway for careers in youth programs, nonprofits and education policy.

## Master of Education

The online M.Ed. in Curriculum and Instruction is for working educators who want to design better courses, use assessment well and lead change in their schools. You'll apply every course to your own classroom or program.

## What you'll learn

- How people learn at different ages and stages
- How to plan lessons, assess learning and adjust your teaching
- How to support multilingual learners and students with disabilities
- How to use technology to make learning more engaging and accessible
- How to partner with families and communities
