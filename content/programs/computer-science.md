Software runs almost everything now, from the grid that powers your home to the apps that schedule your classes. Computer Science at Connected University teaches you how it works underneath, so you can build systems that are fast, reliable and responsible.

## Bachelor of Science

You'll start with programming and data structures, then move into systems, algorithms and theory. In your upper years you choose electives in machine learning, cybersecurity, databases, mobile development or human-computer interaction, and you finish with a team project for a real client.

## Master of Science

The M.S. deepens your expertise in distributed systems, machine learning and software architecture. Full-time students finish in two years; working professionals can study part time online.

## What you'll learn

- How to design, test and maintain software with a team
- How data structures and algorithms shape performance
- How computers, networks and the cloud work together
- How machine learning models are built, evaluated and deployed responsibly
- How to explain technical trade-offs to people who aren't engineers
