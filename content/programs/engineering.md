Engineers turn ideas into things that work. The B.S. in Engineering gives you a strong foundation in math, physics and design, then lets you specialize in the field that fits your goals.

## Three concentrations

- **Mechanical Engineering:** machines, energy systems, robotics and manufacturing
- **Civil & Environmental Engineering:** buildings, transportation, water systems and sustainable infrastructure
- **Electrical Engineering:** circuits, power, electronics and control systems

## Design from day one

In your very first semester you'll work on a team to design and build a working prototype. That hands-on approach continues through the program, ending with a year-long senior design project sponsored by an industry or community partner.

## What you'll learn

- How to apply math and physics to real design problems
- How to model, test and improve a design
- How to work safely in labs and the fabrication shop
- How to manage engineering projects, budgets and schedules
- How engineering decisions affect people and the environment
