Open House is the best way to see Connected University in a single day. Start with a welcome from the admissions team, then build your own schedule:

- **Academic fairs** with faculty from every school, including Environmental Science, Nursing, Engineering and Business
- **Campus tours** led by current students, including residence halls and Sustainability House
- **Sessions on admission and financial aid**, including how merit scholarships work
- **Lunch in the dining hall** and time to ask students anything

Applying Early Action? Open House is a week after the Early Action deadline of {{facts.admissions.deadlines.earlyAction}}, so it's a great time to ask about next steps.
