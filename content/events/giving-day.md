Giving Day is one day when alumni, students, parents, faculty, staff and friends come together for Connected University students. Every gift, of any size, counts, and matching challenges multiply your impact all day.

Register to get a reminder on the day and updates on the challenges as they unlock. On campus, students host a celebration in the Student Center, with thank-you notes for donors and a live leaderboard.

Learn more about [Giving Day](/giving/giving-day/), or [give now](/giving/give/) to get a head start.
