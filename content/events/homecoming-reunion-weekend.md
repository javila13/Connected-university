Every October, alumni and their families come home to Connected University. This year's weekend includes:

- **Friday:** the alumni welcome reception and the Alumni Awards
- **Saturday:** campus tours, faculty talks, a family festival and the Homecoming game, Men's Soccer at Telegraph Field
- **Saturday evening:** milestone class reunion dinners for classes celebrating reunions every {{facts.alumni.reunionEveryYears}} years
- **Sunday:** a farewell brunch and a Young Alumni gathering

See [Events & Reunions](/alumni/events/) for more alumni events throughout the year.
