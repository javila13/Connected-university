Summers are getting hotter, and the neighborhoods with the least shade often feel it most. In this talk, [Dr. Helena Ostrowski](/academics/faculty/helena-ostrowski/), Professor of Sustainability Studies, shares what her research and community projects have learned about cooling neighborhoods: trees, cool surfaces, cooling centers and the community partnerships that make them work.

The Sustainability Lecture Series is free and open to the public. Students, alumni, neighbors and prospective students are all welcome, in person or online. Stay afterward for a reception with Sustainability Studies and Environmental Science students.
