Congratulations, you're in! Admitted Students Day is your chance to picture yourself at Connected University before you decide.

- **Sit in on sample classes** taught by faculty in your program
- **Meet current students** over lunch and on student-led tours
- **Talk with financial aid counselors** about your aid offer
- **Visit residence halls** and learn how to apply for housing by {{facts.admissions.deadlines.housingApplication}}

See [Admitted Students](/admissions/admitted/) for your next steps.
