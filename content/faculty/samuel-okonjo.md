Dr. Okonjo teaches students to build machine learning systems and to ask hard questions about them: who the data represents, how a model can fail, and who is affected when it does.

In the classroom, Dr. Okonjo is known for turning algorithms into puzzles and for projects built on real public datasets. Dr. Okonjo's research group applies machine learning to energy and climate data, such as forecasting building energy use, and studies how to make AI systems more transparent. Undergraduate and master's students work side by side in the group.

Dr. Okonjo also mentors students preparing for internships and teaches in the online M.S. in Computer Science.
