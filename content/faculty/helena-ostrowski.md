Dr. Ostrowski spent years advising companies and cities on sustainability before coming to Connected University, and still brings that practice into every course. Students in the Community Partnership Practicum work in teams on live projects for local businesses, schools and city departments, with Dr. Ostrowski as their project lead.

Dr. Ostrowski also directs the Professional Certificate in Sustainability through Continuing Education. Many certificate learners are Connected U graduates who come back to add sustainability expertise to their careers, and Dr. Ostrowski enjoys seeing former students return.

Dr. Ostrowski's research looks at how organizations decide what to measure, and how neighborhoods can prepare for extreme heat and other climate risks.
