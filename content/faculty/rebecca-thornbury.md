Dr. Thornbury worked as a registered nurse in emergency and community health settings before teaching, and still brings those patient stories into class.

As coordinator of the online RN to B.S.N. program, Dr. Thornbury works with nurses who are balancing full-time shifts with school, and has built the program around that reality: flexible deadlines inside each course, advising in the evening, and assignments that apply directly to students' own workplaces.

Dr. Thornbury's research focuses on community health outreach and on simulation-based teaching that helps new nurses feel ready on their first day.
