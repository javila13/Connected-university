Sustainability is now part of almost every job description, from operations to finance to communications. The Professional Certificate in Sustainability gives you the frameworks and tools to lead that work, in a format built for people who already have full-time jobs.

Courses run one at a time, online or in a hybrid format with occasional Saturday sessions on campus. Every course ends with a project you can apply to your own organization, so you finish with a portfolio, not just a credential.

The certificate is directed by [Dr. Helena Ostrowski](/academics/faculty/helena-ostrowski/), who also leads the Sustainability Studies practicum. Many learners are Connected University graduates who come back to add sustainability expertise to their careers.
