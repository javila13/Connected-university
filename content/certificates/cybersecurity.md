Every organization is a target, and skilled defenders are in short supply. The Professional Certificate in Cybersecurity gives you practical skills to protect systems and data, with hands-on virtual labs in every course.

The certificate suits IT professionals moving into security and career changers with some technical background. Its graduate credits count toward the M.S. in Computer Science if you decide to keep going.
