Every team has more data than it uses. The Professional Certificate in Data Analytics teaches you to find the questions that matter, answer them with data and share the results so people act on them.

You'll work with real public datasets in every course and finish with a portfolio project, often using data from your own job. The certificate is directed by [Dr. Samuel Okonjo](/academics/faculty/samuel-okonjo/), who teaches machine learning in the Computer Science program.
