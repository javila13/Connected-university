Nonprofits do some of the most important work in our communities, often with lean teams and tight budgets. The Professional Certificate in Nonprofit Management gives staff and leaders the tools to plan strategically, raise money and prove their impact.

Each course is built around real nonprofit cases, and you can bring a challenge from your own organization to work on throughout the certificate.
