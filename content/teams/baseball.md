Baseball at Founders Park is a Southern California spring tradition. Head coach Bill Haverford's teams win with deep pitching, solid defense and aggressive baserunning, and the staff led the conference in strikeouts last season.

Founders Park's new dugouts and bullpens were funded entirely by gifts to the Condor Club. Bring the family for Family Day in April, with kids' run-the-bases after the game.
