Women's Volleyball brings some of the loudest nights of the year to Condor Arena. Head coach Lauren Kessler's teams are known for defense and long rallies, and for never giving up a point without a fight.

The team is just as committed off the court. This fall the team spent a bye weekend restoring a coastal wetland alongside Environmental Science students. The October awareness game raises money for cancer research, with the arena dressed in pink.
