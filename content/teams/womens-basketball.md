Women's Basketball wins with defense, rebounding and toughness. Head coach Tasha Greene has built one of the most consistent programs in the conference, with a tournament appearance year after year and a roster that leads all Condors teams in community service.

This season's team is led by the returning Conference Defensive Player of the Year. The January doubleheader with Men's Basketball is one of the biggest nights of the winter at Condor Arena.
