The Condors play a high-pressing, possession style that's made them one of the toughest teams in the conference to break down. Under head coach Dana Whitlock, the program has built its success on a simple idea: develop players who are as committed in the classroom as they are on the field.

Home games at Telegraph Field draw students, families and youth teams from across the region. Come early for pregame tailgates on the lawn, and stay to meet the players after the final whistle.

Off the field, the team runs free youth soccer clinics every fall for local elementary schools, and players mentor first-year student-athletes through their first semester.
