Men's Soccer plays an attacking, quick-passing game built around a young core of players. Head coach Rafael Ortega recruits players from Southern California and around the world, and the team has become a favorite of the student section.

The Homecoming game each October is the biggest home game of the year, with alumni returning for Homecoming & Reunion Weekend, a halftime celebration and a postgame alumni reception.

The team also leads the Condors in the classroom, posting the highest team grade point average of any men's team.
