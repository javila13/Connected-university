Men's Basketball plays fast. Head coach Andre Fontaine's teams push the pace, share the ball and defend for the full court, and with four starters returning, this season's team has its eyes on a conference title.

Condor Arena's student section, known as the Nest, is one of the best atmospheres in the conference. Students get in free with their ID.

Every summer the team runs a youth basketball camp on campus, and players visit local elementary schools during the season to read with students.
