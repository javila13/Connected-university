Last fiscal year brought a record number of first-time gifts to Connected University, and young alumni led the way.

Many first-time donors chose to support the scholarships and tutoring programs that helped them when they were students. "I remember exactly which semester almost didn't work out, and who helped," one recent graduate wrote with a first gift. "I wanted to be that for someone else."

First gifts of every size add up. Together, they fund emergency grants, study sessions for the toughest courses and scholarships for students who might not otherwise enroll.
