A team of Computer Science undergraduate and master's students in [Dr. Samuel Okonjo's](/academics/faculty/samuel-okonjo/) research group has built a machine learning model that forecasts energy use in campus buildings a day ahead.

The model combines weather forecasts, class schedules and past energy data. Facilities staff are using it to plan when to pre-cool buildings, shifting energy use to times when it's cheaper and cleaner.

"The students treated the facilities team as their client," said Dr. Okonjo. "They built something people actually use, and they learned to explain what the model can and can't tell you."

The project grew out of Machine Learning (CS 4500) and continues as a capstone. Learn more about [Computer Science](/academics/programs/computer-science/).
