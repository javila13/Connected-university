Students living in Sustainability House celebrated the opening of a new rooftop garden this month, funded by gifts from the Sustainability Alumni Network.

The garden's raised beds grow vegetables and herbs for the campus food pantry, and a rainwater system supplies most of its water. Students plan and run the garden themselves, and they're already hosting workshops for local elementary schools on composting and growing food.

"It's the kind of project that ties everything together," said one Sustainability Studies student who helped design the garden. "We learned about it in class, we planned it in the practicum, and now we're feeding people with it."

Sustainability House is one of Connected University's [living-learning communities](/student-success/campus-life/). See how gifts like this one make a difference in [Impact Stories](/giving/impact/).
