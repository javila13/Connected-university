Women's Volleyball spent its bye weekend ankle-deep in a coastal wetland, pulling invasive plants and planting native grasses alongside Environmental Science students and faculty.

The project was organized with the School of Science & Sustainability, whose students monitor water quality at the site as part of their coursework. Players learned how to identify native species and test water samples, and Environmental Science students explained how restored wetlands filter runoff before it reaches the ocean.

"It was a different kind of teamwork," said head coach Lauren Kessler. "Our players got to see what their classmates study, and they were proud to leave something better than they found it."

The team plans to make the service weekend a fall tradition. The Condors close the regular season at home on Senior Night at Condor Arena.
