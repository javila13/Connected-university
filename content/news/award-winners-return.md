Wilma Grace (YSS ’18) and Courtney Tran-Kim (YSS ’22), winners of the Gracelines International Sustainability Award, came back to campus this month to co-teach a session of Foundations of Sustainability, the course where both of them started.

Wilma Grace talked with students about leading climate-resilience projects with city governments, and Courtney Tran-Kim walked through how companies measure and report their environmental impact. Both stayed after class to answer questions about internships and careers.

"Sitting in that room again was a reminder of how much this place gave us," Courtney Tran-Kim said. "We want students to know the path is real."
