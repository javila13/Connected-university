Winter is almost here, and so is basketball at Condor Arena. Women's Basketball opens its season at home, followed the next night by Men's Basketball, and both teams have reason for optimism.

Men's Basketball returns four starters from a team that reached last season's conference tournament semifinals. "We want to play fast and play together," said head coach Andre Fontaine. "Our guys know what's possible this year."

Women's Basketball is led by the returning Conference Defensive Player of the Year, and head coach Tasha Greene's team has reached the conference tournament year after year.
