The Condors are champions. Two second-half goals carried Women's Soccer to a 2–0 win at Canyon State University on Saturday, clinching the {{facts.athletics.conference}} regular-season title with a game to spare.

"This group has bought into each other from the first day of preseason," said head coach Dana Whitlock. "They defend for each other, they run for each other, and today they got the reward."

The title caps the longest unbeaten run in program history and gives the Condors home-field advantage in the conference tournament. The semifinal is at Telegraph Field, and students get in free with their ID.

The team's success goes beyond the scoreboard. Players ran free youth soccer clinics for local elementary schools again this fall, and the team carries one of the highest grade point averages of any Condors program.
