The Tutoring & Learning Center has extended its online hours into the evening, so students who work during the day, including many online and RN to B.S.N. students, can get help when they need it.

The expansion includes evening online sessions for Organic Chemistry and Calculus, two of the courses where students most often ask for support. Sessions are led by peer tutors who recently took the courses.

"A lot of our students are working, commuting or caring for family," said the center's director. "If they can only study at nine at night, that's when we should be there."

Students can book sessions in the [Student Portal](/portal/). Learn more about [tutoring](/student-success/tutoring/).
